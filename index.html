📋 Upload Guidelines

  • Photo: Recent (last 6 months), passport-size, white background, JPG/PNG.
  • Marksheet: All marks clearly visible. JPG, PNG or PDF.
  • Aadhaar Card: Name & 12-digit number clearly legible. JPG, PNG or PDF.
  • Maximum file size: 5MB per document.
  • Blurry or illegible documents may result in application rejection.
